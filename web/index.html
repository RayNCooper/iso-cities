iso-cities

Any city or postal code, drawn as isometric pixel art from real OpenStreetMap data. Everything below runs in your browser — no server, no upload.

What you are looking at

The place is geocoded with Nominatim, the buildings, roads, water and green space around it come from the Overpass API, and every footprint is extruded to its real height and drawn back to front in a 2:1 isometric projection. The rasteriser and the bitmap font are part of the library; there is no canvas drawing API involved beyond handing over the finished pixels.

A smaller radius means more pixels per metre, so buildings get chunkier and windows appear. Larger areas mean a lot more map data — the public Overpass API is the slow part, not the renderer.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the ODbL. Images you save keep that credit drawn in. Please be gentle with the public APIs — requests are rate limited to one per second and cached in memory.
