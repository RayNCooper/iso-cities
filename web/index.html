What you are looking at

The place is geocoded with Nominatim, the buildings, roads, water and green space around it come from the Overpass API, and every footprint is extruded to its real height and drawn back to front in a 2:1 isometric projection. The rasteriser and the bitmap font are part of the library; no canvas drawing API is involved beyond handing over finished pixels.

Map data is fetched once and kept, so changing the theme, the size or the shadows re-renders from what is already in memory. Only the place, the detail level and the highlight need a new download.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the ODbL. Images you save keep that credit drawn in. Please be gentle with the public APIs — requests are rate limited to one per second.
